How It Scores

GOAT points follow the manager, not the franchise name. Finalized season awards, regular-season wins (0.5 each), winners-bracket playoff wins, runner-up, 3rd place, Good Season bonuses, and 10+ win streaks count. Live wins and win streaks count as they happen; accolades and Good Seasons only land when the season locks. A Good Season is a regular-season win rate of 61% or better (NBA 50-win pace). A 10+ streak is rare enough to pay on its own. The GOAT title itself is worth 0 — it is the scoreboard, not another stackable award.
