How It Scores

GOAT points follow the manager, not the franchise name. Scoring stacks in three lanes: playoff accolades first, then yearly awards, then regular-season grind. Live wins and win streaks count as they happen; yearly awards and Good Seasons only land when the season locks. Career lineup efficiency is shown for context (same metric as Coach of the Year) but awards no GOAT points. The GOAT title itself is worth 0 — it is the scoreboard, not another stackable award.
