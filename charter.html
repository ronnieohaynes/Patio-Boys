Article I

Buy-in & Dues

Entry is $50 per franchise, due before the reconstruction draft and again before the commissioner approves any trade. No exceptions, no IOUs.

The commissioner personally confirms a franchise is paid up before signing off on a trade involving that franchise. Verbal promises don't count.

Article II

Roster & Keepers

There's no pre-draft keeper deadline and no forced cutdown. Every franchise carries its full current roster straight into the reconstruction draft, then adds up to 5 more picks on top. Rosters get trimmed down to the active limit on each manager's own schedule afterward, the same way roster management always works, any time before Week 1 lineups lock. Taxi and IR still apply as usual.

Taxi

Active roster is 18 (10 starters + 8 bench). The 2 taxi spots are extra stash slots for players who have played fewer than 3 NBA seasons — they don’t burn an active roster spot. IR is separate for qualifying injuries. No one is required to cut before draft day; free agency and preseason can play out first, and the wire (and FAAB) handles the rest.

Article III

The Reconstruction Draft

jaysonarban (Arban) and Sad Face Fried Rice (Jeff) are leaving the league. Their rosters enter the free agent pool. Their own draft picks for this year are voided outright, no lottery, no consolation. Sleeper requires all 8 teams to hold a round 1 pick, no exceptions, so picks 7 and 8 get entered as Hamas and Belt, who get nothing from this round, purely so the commissioner can immediately trade those two picks away. Every round after round 1 auto-snakes automatically.

Article IV

The Lottery

Picks 1 and 2 come down to a single coin flip between Funeral Home and BoobieDominguez, 50/50, the actual #1 and #2 overall picks on the line, outright. Picks 3 through 6 are an open lottery among Bam Add the Mayo, Champs, Papa Book, and Freakonomics, weighted by current Max Points, worst ceiling gets the best odds. Picks 7 and 8 are entered as Hamas and Belt purely because Sleeper requires every team to hold a pick, then immediately traded, pick 7 to the coin-flip loser, pick 8 to the coin-flip winner. Round 2 then auto-snakes, except for three standing trades: Belt's pick always goes to Freakonomics, Hamas and Papa Book have already swapped theirs, and Freakonomics' own pick gets re-lotteried among the field since they already have a second pick guaranteed.

Article V

Playoffs

Top 4 franchises qualify. Playoffs open in week 18, the short week coming off the All-Star break. Each round is a two-week matchup, combined score decides the winner. Ties go to the higher seed.

Dynasty value uses the same formula as the Trade Analyzer (proj/history blend, age, injury). GM compares each franchise’s total roster dynasty score at season end vs the prior season end — biggest gain wins. In the league’s first season (no prior year), GM is the highest end-of-season dynasty total instead. Lineup efficiency is actual starter fantasy points divided by the optimal legal lineup from that week’s starters + bench (Sleeper basketball Max PF style — taxi and IR/reserve do not count), averaged across scored weeks. GOAT follows the manager across playoff accolades, yearly awards, regular-season milestones (win / playoff-win / PF bundles, Good Seasons, 10+ streaks), and lead bonuses (+1 each for leading or tying Champ, Runner-up, 3rd, Playoff wins, MVP, Coach, GM, MIP, Good Season, Win streak, RS wins, career Points For, and career Avg PF). The GOAT title itself is worth 0 and lives on the GOAT Race.
