Roster Rating

Max Points mirrors Sleeper's utilization idea. It pulls every week of last season's stats, finds each player's mean and standard deviation of weekly fantasy output under your real scoring settings, then sums the top 10 players' (mean + 1 standard deviation), a realistic ceiling for a week where the manager starts the right lineup. Score is graded as Max Points / 500 on a traditional school scale (97% A+ · 93% A · 90% A- · 87% B+ · 83% B · 80% B- · 70% C- · 60% D- · below F) — not curved against the rest of the league. Says nothing about injuries, role changes, or unproven rookies.
